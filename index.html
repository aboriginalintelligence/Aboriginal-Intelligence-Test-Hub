No. 01. Agreement

Candidate Commitment

A candidate downloads the commitment, reads the codes, signs it, and gives members a signed copy. A candidate who takes a seat and does not follow it agrees to resign.

  1. Download the commitment yourself.
  2. Read it.
  3. Commit to it.
  4. Endorse it.
  5. Sign it.
  6. Promote it.
  7. Make it public.
  8. Make a video of yourself saying you signed it and you mean it.
  9. Give a signed copy to members.
