FORT McMURRAY No. 468 FIRST NATION | DOCUMENT 02 OF 20

For the Chief and every Councillor of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation

Form for use at swearing-in | September 30, 2026

StatusPrescribed form of oath under section 6 and Schedule C of the Customary Election Regulations, 2026 (Document 05). Until those Regulations are in force, an elected person may take this oath voluntarily.
TakenAt a meeting open to Band Members, within fourteen (14) days after the declaration of results, before the Electoral Officer, a Commissioner for Oaths, or a Justice of the Peace.
EffectNo person may exercise any power of the office, or vote at a meeting of Council, before taking this oath (Document 05, section 6.2).
FiledWith the Electoral Officer, and published on the website of the First Nation within seven (7) days.

Part 2: The Oath

2.1 The elected person shall read the following aloud, or repeat it after the administering officer. A person who prefers not to swear an oath shall solemnly affirm, and the words "swear" and "affirm" have the same effect. The oath is as follows:

I, ______________________________, having been elected to the office of ______________________________ of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation, do solemnly [swear / affirm] that I will faithfully, honestly, and impartially carry out the duties of that office for the benefit of all Band Members, on and off the Reserves. I will uphold the Customary Election Regulations, the Council Procedure By-law, the Conduct and Conflict of Interest Code, and the other laws of the First Nation. I will make decisions only at duly convened meetings of Council, I will disclose every conflict of interest and remove myself from any matter in which I have one, and I will not use my office, or the property, funds, or employees of the First Nation, for the benefit of myself, my family, or my friends. I will make the information that belongs to the Band Members available to them as the law of the First Nation requires, and I will not direct, pressure, or retaliate against any employee, Band Member, or official who is carrying out their duties. I understand that the authority of this office is held in trust for the Band Members of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation.

Part 3: Acknowledgements

3.1 By signing, the elected person acknowledges that:

  • (a) the powers of Council may be exercised only by Resolution at a duly convened meeting of Council at which a quorum is present, as paragraph 2(3)(b) of the Indian Act and the laws of the First Nation require;
  • (b) no Portfolio gives them authority to direct employees, approve spending, or bind the First Nation;
  • (c) they must file a statement of interests with the Integrity Commissioner within thirty (30) days after taking office, and update it every year and whenever it changes;
  • (d) they must publish three (3) to five (5) measurable goals for their Portfolio within thirty (30) days after taking office, and report on them every quarter;
  • (e) they must not hold, enter, or renew a contract with the First Nation or a First Nation Business Entity while in office, except to complete a contract disclosed before taking office;
  • (f) they must declare every conflict of interest and leave the meeting for the discussion and vote, and a vote on their own pay, expenses, contract, loan, job, or house, or that of their Immediate Family, is void;
  • (g) they must complete orientation training on the Customary Election Regulations, the Council Procedure By-law, the Conduct and Conflict of Interest Code, the Financial Administration Code, and the Spending and Contracts Code within ninety (90) days;
  • (h) Council has no authority to postpone or cancel an election, and a person who votes for or directs such a step may be removed from office;
  • (i) within seven (7) days after leaving office, they must return every record, key, device, password, payment card, and other property of the First Nation in their possession; and
  • (j) the criminal law, including the offences of fraud, theft, and breach of trust, applies to members of Council in the same way it applies to everyone else.
