FORT McMURRAY No. 468 FIRST NATION | DOCUMENT 19 OF 20

Short form of the Candidate Commitment Agreement

For the next election of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation | September 30, 2026

StatusVoluntary. Not a condition of nomination.
RelationshipA shorter version of Document 01, the Candidate Commitment Agreement. A candidate who signs Document 01 does not need to sign this Pledge.

Part 1: Purpose

1.1 This Pledge sets out commitments a candidate makes to the Members of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation before the election.

Part 2: Commitments

2. If Elected, I Pledge To:

2.1 Election Regulations. Within forty-five (45) days after taking office, vote to present the Customary Election Regulations, 2026 to the Electors under section 20 of the Regulations effective May 31, 2014.

2.2 Council composition. Vote to present to the Electors a Council of one Chief and seven Councillors, each elected to a portfolio, including a Councillor who lives off the Reserves, and support an election for the new Seats within one hundred and twenty (120) days after the Regulations are approved.

2.3 Open meetings and records. Within thirty (30) days after taking office, vote to open Council meetings to Members and to publish minutes within twenty-one (21) days and every Band Council Resolution within fourteen (14) days.

2.4 Financial transparency. Vote to publish quarterly financial reports within forty-five (45) days after each quarter, and the audited financial statements with the schedule of Council pay and expenses within one hundred and twenty (120) days after year-end.

2.5 Independent audit and finance committee. Support an auditor appointed by the Electors on the recommendation of a Finance and Audit Committee chaired by an independent accountant, with the results presented to all Members.

2.6 Competitive bidding. Vote for public tenders on all contracts over fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00), a public Contract Registry for contracts over ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00), and published award decisions.

2.7 Conflicts of interest. Disclose my interests in writing within thirty (30) days, leave the room on any matter in which I have a conflict, never vote on my own pay or benefits, and hold no contract with the First Nation while in office.

2.8 Independent decisions. Leave decisions on individual housing, education, loans, membership, and hiring to the persons and bodies named in the governance documents of the First Nation, with appeals to an Appeals Panel independent of Council.

2.9 No extensions. Never vote to postpone or cancel an election or to extend my own term.

2.10 Mandate and reporting. Within thirty (30) days after taking office, publish three (3) to five (5) specific, measurable goals for my portfolio, report on them in writing every quarter, and answer Members' questions at an Accountability Session every quarter.

Part 3: Acknowledgement

3.1 I acknowledge that the Members have a right to hold me to these commitments, and that failure to pursue them in good faith within the times stated is a matter the Members may raise through every accountability process available under the laws of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation.
