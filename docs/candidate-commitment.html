FORT McMURRAY No. 468 FIRST NATION | DOCUMENT 01 OF 20

A voluntary public undertaking by a candidate for Chief or Councillor

For the next election of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation | September 30, 2026

StatusVoluntary. Signing is not a condition of nomination under the Customary Election Regulations effective May 31, 2014, and the Electoral Officer cannot require it.
PurposeA written, witnessed record of what a candidate will do if elected, and by when.
PublicationSigned Agreements are published without the candidate's telephone number, email, or address.
Short formDocument 19, the Candidate Accountability Pledge.

Part 1: Nature of this Agreement

1.1 This Agreement is a voluntary public undertaking by a candidate for office in the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation, made to the Members of the First Nation.

1.2 This Agreement is not a contract that requires an elected person to vote in a particular way.

1.3 In this Agreement, the "Governance Documents" means the following draft Documents, dated September 30, 2026, as they may be revised after community review:

  • (a) Document 03, the Governance Constitution, and Document 04, the Members' Bill of Rights;
  • (b) Document 05, the Customary Election Regulations, 2026;
  • (c) Document 06, the Council Procedure By-law, 2026;
  • (d) Document 07, the Conduct and Conflict of Interest Code, 2026;
  • (e) Document 08, the Membership Code, 2026;
  • (f) Documents 09 and 10, the Financial Administration Code, 2026 and the Spending and Contracts Code, 2026;
  • (g) Documents 11, 12, and 13, the Housing Code, 2026, the Education Support Code, 2026, and the Business Ventures and Member Loans Code, 2026;
  • (h) Documents 14, 15, and 16, the Access to Information and Privacy Code, 2026, the Whistleblower Protection Code, 2026, and the Appeals and Complaints Code, 2026; and
  • (i) Documents 17 and 18, the Community Votes and Petitions Code, 2026 and the Administration and Personnel Code, 2026.

1.4 In this Agreement, the "2014 Regulations" means the Customary Election Regulations of the Fort McMurray No. 468 First Nation effective May 31, 2014, and the "2014 By-law" means By-law No. 2014.2, the Council Accountability and Procedures By-law.

Part 3: What I Understand

3.1 By signing, I confirm that I understand the following:

  • (a) The authority of the office I am seeking belongs to the Members. If elected, I will hold it in trust for all of them, on and off the Reserves, and not for myself, my family, or my friends.
  • (b) Council can act only by Resolution at a duly convened meeting with a quorum present, as paragraph 2(3)(b) of the Indian Act provides. No member of Council can adopt any law alone.
  • (c) The Customary Election Regulations can be changed only in the way section 20 of the 2014 Regulations requires: Council approves the draft by Resolution, gives at least fourteen (14) days' posted and mailed notice with the full text attached, and a Special Meeting of Electors with at least fifty percent (50%) of the number who voted in the previous Election present approves it by secret ballot.
  • (d) The Membership Code can be changed only in the way its own amendment section requires, which includes the consent of a majority of all Electors.
  • (e) Council can replace the 2014 By-law by motion under its section 26.1, and can adopt the other Codes among the Governance Documents by Resolution, without waiting for a vote of the Electors. The Electors then ratify them.
  • (f) The Governance Constitution and the Members' Bill of Rights take effect only if a majority of the Electors vote in a Fundamental Vote and a majority of those voting approve.
  • (g) The fraud, theft, and breach of trust provisions of the criminal law apply to elected officials as they apply to everyone.
  • (h) This Agreement will be published.

Part 4: My Commitments if Elected

4. Within Thirty (30) Days After I Take Office

4.1 I will move, or second, and vote for a motion to enact the Council Procedure By-law, 2026 in place of the 2014 By-law under its section 26.1, and to publish it under section 86 of the Indian Act.

4.2 I will move, or second, and vote for a Resolution adopting the Conduct and Conflict of Interest Code, the Financial Administration Code, the Spending and Contracts Code, the Housing Code, the Education Support Code, the Business Ventures and Member Loans Code, the Access to Information and Privacy Code, the Whistleblower Protection Code, the Appeals and Complaints Code, the Community Votes and Petitions Code, and the Administration and Personnel Code.

4.3 I will move, or second, and vote for a Resolution under section 20.2 of the 2014 Regulations approving the Customary Election Regulations, 2026 as Council's draft amendments.

4.4 I will publish three (3) to five (5) measurable goals for my portfolio, and report on them in writing every quarter.

4.5 I will file a statement of my employment, business interests, and every contract or relationship I or my Immediate Family have with the First Nation or its business entities.

5. Within Forty-Five (45) Days After I Take Office

5.1 I will vote to post and mail the notice required by sections 20.3 to 20.5 of the 2014 Regulations, and to hold the Special Meeting of Electors no more than thirty (30) days after the notice is posted.

5.2 I will vote to fund the Special Meeting properly, including transportation for Non-resident Electors, childcare, and Elder support.

5.3 I will vote to call a Fundamental Vote on the Governance Constitution and the Members' Bill of Rights, conducted by an independent electoral officer with mail-in voting for every Elector.

5.4 I will support the written request of Electors under section 13 of the Membership Code, and will vote to prepare and present the Membership Code, 2026 and to hold the consent vote after the required notice.

6. After the Election Regulations Are Ratified

6.1 I will vote to appoint the standing Electoral Officer, the Election Appeal Tribunal roster, the Integrity Commissioner, and the Chair of the Appeals Panel within the times the Governance Documents set.

6.2 I will not vote to delay the Transitional Election for the new Council Seats, and I will cooperate with the Electoral Officer to hold it within one hundred and twenty (120) days.

6.3 I will vote to put every Code adopted under section 4.2 to the Electors for ratification within twelve (12) months.

7. For My Whole Term

7.1 I will not vote for, direct, or support any step to postpone or cancel an election, or to extend my own term.

7.2 I will make decisions only at duly convened meetings of Council, and I will not sign any Band Council Resolution for a decision that was not made at such a meeting.

7.3 I will declare every conflict of interest and leave the room for the discussion and vote, and I will not vote on my own pay, expenses, contract, loan, job, or house, or those of my family.

7.4 I will not hold, enter, or renew any contract with the First Nation or its business entities while in office, except to complete a contract I disclosed before taking office.

7.5 I will vote to publish Council minutes within twenty-one (21) days, every Band Council Resolution within fourteen (14) days, quarterly financial reports within forty-five (45) days, a Contract Registry of every contract over ten thousand dollars ($10,000.00), and the annual audited financial statements within one hundred and twenty (120) days.

7.6 I will vote for public tenders on contracts over fifty thousand dollars ($50,000.00), and for a vote of the Electors on unbudgeted commitments over five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000.00).

7.7 I will not use First Nation staff, vehicles, facilities, funds, or communications for my personal benefit or my campaign.

7.8 I will not contact the person or body deciding an individual Member's housing, education support, loan, membership, or job application, except to ask in writing about its status at the Member's request.

7.9 I will attend an Accountability Session with Members in every quarter, on a Reserve and online, with no screening of questions.

7.10 Within seven (7) days after I leave office, I will return every record, key, device, password, payment card, and other property of the First Nation in my possession.

Part 5: Personal Undertaking to Resign

8. Undertaking

8.1 If, by the forty-fifth (45th) day after I take office, I have not moved or seconded and voted for each of the motions in sections 4.1, 4.3, and 5.1, I will deliver a signed Notice of Resignation to Council under section 16 of the 2014 Regulations within seven (7) days, effective no more than thirty (30) days after delivery.

8.2 This undertaking does not apply if I brought forward or seconded each motion and voted for it, and it failed because other members of Council voted against it, refused to attend, or denied quorum. In that case I will publish, within seven (7) days, a written account of what happened and how each member voted.

8.3 I understand that this undertaking is personal and does not take effect automatically.
