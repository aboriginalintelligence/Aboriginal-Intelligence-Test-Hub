Respectful use

This is a historical photography / studio scenery tool. Your face and body cutout stay intact and recognizable. Only the background is replaced with 1800s-era studio or painted period scenery. Optional light sepia/grain on the subject does not morph ethnicity, face shape, or clothing.

Not a costume, ethnicity, or Indigenous stereotype generator. No period Indigenous clothing overlays, headdresses, face morphing, or “look like an 1800s Indian” features — those are out of scope. Living people are not props. Use respectfully.

Privacy: Your photo is processed in this browser (cutout + composite). It is not uploaded to this hub’s servers. The background-removal library may download model files from a CDN on first use and cache them locally. You share a result only if you download it.
